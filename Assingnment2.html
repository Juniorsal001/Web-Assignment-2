About Us

Salone Health Connect is a Digital Public Good designed to improve Healthcare accessibility in Sierra Leone. Our platform helps citizens locate nearby hospitals,clinics, pharmacies and emergency services quickly and efficiently.

Our mission is to use technology to connect communities with realiable healthcare information and support healthier lives.
